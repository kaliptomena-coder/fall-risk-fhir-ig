Structures: Resource Profiles

These define constraints on FHIR resources for systems conforming to this implementation guide.

Fall Risk Factor Observation

A standardized FHIR Observation representing a single contributing factor to fall risk (e.g., fear of falling, walking ability, medication use). This profile supports both automated EHR data extraction and manual questionnaire responses, providing a consistent structure for clinical risk scoring algorithms.
Fall Risk Observation

The outcome of a fall risk screening episode, capturing the overall risk classification (Low / Moderate / High) and referencing the aggregated score Observation.
Fall Risk Performance Test Observation

Objective physical performance measurements used in fall risk assessment, including the Sit to stand frequency in 30 seconds, 4-Stage Balance Test, and the Timed Up & Go (TUG) test.
Fall Risk Score Observation

An aggregated fall risk score (0–34) derived from individual Fall Risk Factor Observations and Fall Risk Performance Observations. The 'hasMember' element links to the contributing factors, ensuring full traceability of the clinical evidence.

Terminology: Value Sets

These define sets of codes used by systems conforming to this implementation guide.

Fall Risk Category ValueSet

Risk classification outcomes for fall risk assessment using SNOMED qualifier values.
Fall Risk Factors ValueSet

Standardized LOINC and SNOMED codes for fall risk assessment inputs (factors 1–10).
Fall Risk Performance Tests ValueSet

Codes for objective physical performance tests used in fall risk assessment (tests a–c).
Fall Risk Score Threshold ValueSet

Local codes that identify which score band (Low / Moderate / High) a computed FallRiskScore falls into. Used in FallRiskObservation.note or as an interpretation code alongside valueQuantity in FallRiskScoreObservation.

Terminology: Code Systems

These define new code systems used by systems conforming to this implementation guide.

Fall Risk Local Code System

Local codes for physical performance tests, aggregate scores, and fall risk factors not available in LOINC or SNOMED CT.

Terminology: Concept Maps

These define transformations to convert between codes by systems conforming with this implementation guide.

Fall Risk Score Threshold ConceptMap

Maps local score-band codes (score-low / score-moderate / score-high) to the corresponding SNOMED risk category codes in FallRiskCategoryVS. The numeric cutoffs encoded in the comments and group.element.display fields are the authoritative thresholds for this IG: score-low = total score 0–11 score-moderate = total score 12–22 score-high = total score 23–34
